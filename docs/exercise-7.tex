\section{Formula 1 Tracks}
NOTE THIS section is a WORK IN PROGRESS
The $ABC 123 XYZ$ city has been asked to be a location in new formula 1 season.
The major has tasked you to find a optimal route throughout the city. They have
assembled the following requirements for the route:
\begin{itemize}
\item All cars must use existing roads. The city does not wish to build more roads,
  nor can they destroy existing buildings.
\item You must visit $m$ out of $n$ special locations throughout the city.
\item The length of the circuit must be between $k$ and $l$.
\item To prevent injuries and deaths the circuit may never cross itself.
\item It must have between $a$ and $b$ corners.
\item As they need to race 50 laps around it, the start location and end location
  need to be the same place. (The circuit needs to be a full circle.)
\item For each street you need permits and possible renovations you are tasked to minimize the total cost for the race.

\end{itemize}

\newcommand\ite[3]{\mathtt{ite}(#1,#2,#3)}

We identify the variables

\begin{itemize}
\item $m = 4$ is the amount of minimum point of interests the track 'visits'
\item $n = 6$ is the total amount of point of interests in the city
\item $a = 7$ is the minimum amount of corners
\item $b = 24$ is the maximum amount of corners
\item $l = 1500m$ is the minimum length of the track
\item $k = 6000m$ is the maximum length of the track
\item $\alpha_{e} \in \mathbb{B}$ representing whether the road $e$ is part of the track
\item $\beta_{e,e'} \in \mathbb{B}$ representing whether the roads $e$, and $e'$ contain a point of intertests
\item $\delta_{e} \in \mathbb{N}$ representing the distance of the road $e$
\item $\gamma_{e,e'} \in \mathbb{N}$ representing whether there is a corner between $e$ and $e'$

\end{itemize}

\subsubsection{Sets}
\begin{itemize}
\item The set of intersections: $V$
\item The set of intersections adjecent to edge $e$: $V_e$
\item The set of roads adjecent to intersection $v$: $E_v$
\item The set of points of intertests: $S \subseteq V$
\end{itemize}

The track does not cross it self, that is each intersection has either 0 or 2 roads on the track
\[
	\bigwedge_{v \in V} (\sum_{e \in E_v} \ite{\alpha_e}{1}{0} = 0 \vee \sum_{e \in V_e} \ite{\alpha_e}{1}{0} = 2)
\]
The track visits at least $m$ special locations
\[
\sum_{(e,e') \in E \times E}(\ite{\alpha_{e} \wedge \alpha_{e'} \wedge \beta_{e,e'}}{1}{0}) \ge m
\]
There are between $a$ and $b$ corners
\[
a \leq \sum_{(e,e') \in E \times E} \ite{\alpha_{e} \wedge \alpha_{e'} \wedge \gamma_{e,e'}}{1}{0}) \leq b
\]

The track length is between $k$ and $l$
\[
k \leq \sum_{e \in E} \ite{\alpha_{e} }{\delta_{e}}{0} \leq l
\]


Options
Minimize cost of roads:
\[
Mininimze \sum_{1 \leq i \leq s}\sum_{(v,v') \in (V \times V)} \ite{\alpha_{v,i-1} \wedge \alpha_{v', i}}{c_{v,v'}}{0} 
\]
Minimize internal cost - each internal block has a wheight.
For that we have assert for the internal blocks
\[
\bigwedge_{0 \leq i < s}(\bigvee_{(v, v')\in (V \times V)} (\alpha_{v,i-1} \wedge \alpha_{v', i} \wedge ()))b
\]
\[
Mininimze \sum_{1 \leq i \leq s}\sum_{(v,v') \in (V \times V)} \ite{\alpha_{v,i-1} \wedge \alpha_{v', i}}{c_{v,v'}}{0}
